🎵 AI Music Generator

A small LSTM neural network learns from folk melodies right in your browser, then composes new music.

Step 1: Train the AI

It learns note patterns from 6 classic melodies (transposed into 12 keys). Takes about 20 to 60 seconds.

Not trained yet.

Step 2: Generate music

Low creativity gives safe, repetitive tunes. High creativity gives surprising (and sometimes odd) ones.

Model: LSTM (64 units) in TensorFlow.js. Sound: Web Audio. Everything runs on your device.
